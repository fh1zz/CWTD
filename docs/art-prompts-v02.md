方式：内置image_gen；用途：Unity原创临时角色图集，35个阶段外形，5列×7行。输出计划接入Assets/Resources/Art/pet-stages-v2.png；原units-atlas.png保持不变供敌人／扭蛋机使用。

首轮生成提示词：

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production Unity sprite atlas, transparent PNG. Create EXACTLY 35 original friendly fantasy pet evolution sprites, in a strict uniform grid of FIVE columns and SEVEN rows. Portrait sheet aspect ratio 5:7, equal SQUARE cells, no margins outside the grid. Each creature is centered within its own cell, fully visible, with 12% transparent padding on all sides; NO part may overlap another cell. No text, numbers, labels, lines, borders, scenery, shadow rectangles or checkerboard baked into pixels. Genuine alpha transparency. Crisp thick dark brown outlines, warm hand-painted storybook fantasy tower-defense art, charming but progressively majestic; three-quarter view facing slightly right; readable at small game scale. Do not copy copyrighted character designs. Columns left to right show Stage1 baby, Stage2 growing, Stage3 awakened, Stage4 exalted, Stage5 sovereign of SAME pet identity per row; not mere recoloring: each stage visibly changes silhouette/accessories and elemental power, preserving species.
Row1 GOLD FALCON: tiny round golden fledgling; adolescent with metallic silver-gold feather tips; battle falcon with split blade-shaped wing feathers and helm crest; majestic soaring sun falcon with long streaming tail plumes; regal golden falcon sovereign with a radiant fan of sun-like blades behind wings.
Row2 LEAF SPROUT SPIRIT: tiny bright-green seedling creature with one leaf; round sapling with flower bud; thorn-armored leafy woodland guardian; tall ancient vine sage with branch arms and blossoms; wise great forest spirit with compact tree-crown canopy and glowing green seed-heart. Stay recognizably plant creature, NOT a deer or turtle.
Row3 AQUA OTTER: small round cyan otter with a bubble; sleek young river otter with fin-like scarf; frosty otter mage holding small ice bubbles; larger otter guardian with curved icy shoulder fins and two watery orbs; regal azure otter sovereign with a wave-shaped crown and flowing water-tail arc. Clearly otter face and whiskers in all five.
Row4 FIRE FOX: tiny orange fox cub with one flame-tip tail; flame fox with stronger flaming collar and two tails; fire-ring fox with three spreading tails; majestic red fox with five fan tails and glowing runic collar; fox sovereign with a broad radiant fan of nine flame tails, bounded safely in cell. No smoke hiding face.
Row5 ROCK TURTLE: baby tan turtle with smooth stone shell; mossy armored turtle; dense crystalline rock-plated guardian turtle; mountain turtle with layered stone ridges and moss; sovereign ancient giant turtle with miniature jagged mountain crest on shell and golden seams. Compact four-legged turtle silhouette, not humanoid.
Row6 LIGHT DEER: small pale-gold fawn with tiny glowing antler buds; graceful young deer with branch antlers and dew gems; sacred golden-antler deer with chest star; celestial deer with elongated branching antlers and ribbons of light; majestic ivory-gold deer sovereign with luminous tree-like crown antlers and floral light ornaments. Keep quadruped anatomy.
Row7 VIOLET BAT: tiny round lavender bat with short wings; moon-eared purple bat with crescent marking; hunter bat with sharper layered wing tips and silver eye markings; large midnight bat with layered cape-like wings and violet collar; regal violet bat sovereign with broad scalloped wings, crescent crown and sparkling violet wing veins. Friendly determined faces, no gore.
Every row uses clearly consistent identity and palette. Early stages occupy 55-65% of each cell, later stages 75%; same ground baseline. The five stage poses vary just enough to show growth. Render only these 35 sprites on transparency, no extra props outside cells.
```
